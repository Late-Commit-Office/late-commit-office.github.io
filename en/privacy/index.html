Privacy notice

Effective: 13 November 2026

1. Terms used in this notice

  • “The Office”, “we”, “us” and “our”: The Late Commit Office, operated by the individual named in the Imprint.
  • “You”: anyone who visits the Website or uses the Software.
  • “The Website”: latecommitoffice.com.
  • “The Software”: the programs listed in the Register of Software.

2. Who is responsible for your data

For the purposes of data protection law, The Late Commit Office is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

We process personal data only where necessary to operate the Website, respond to enquiries and support requests, or comply with legal obligations.

The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in running the Website and answering the people who write to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contact: contact[at]latecommitoffice.com

3. The Website

  • The Website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • If you choose a theme, the choice is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and you may remove it at any time.
  • The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information necessary to provide the Website, such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and access times. For details, please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. The Software

Downloads are served by GitHub and are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

gitid

gitid runs entirely on your computer. It only reads from and writes to your local Git configuration as instructed by you. It does not transmit any information to us or any third party.

HDR Shield

HDR Shield runs entirely on your Mac. It only sends a custom gamma table to your screen, collects no data, and transmits nothing to us or any third party.

[Satellite wallpaper app]

To be completed before release. It will name the imagery provider, which receives image requests and therefore your IP address.

5. Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply. Email sent to us is forwarded using Cloudflare Email Routing, which processes the message for delivery. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain support records, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Under data protection law, including the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may also object to or restrict certain processing activities. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact[at]latecommitoffice.com. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

7. Changes

Changes to this notice are published on this page with a new effective date, and recorded in the History of the Office.
